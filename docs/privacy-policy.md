# mVault Privacy Policy (draft)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-19

## Summary

mVault is a zero-knowledge password manager. We store your **account email**, **authentication metadata**, and **encrypted vault blob**. We cannot read your saved logins.

## Data we collect

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Email address | Account identification and sign-in |
| Password hash | Account authentication (not your master password) |
| Encrypted vault blob | Sync across your devices |
| Technical logs | Service reliability (no vault contents) |

## Data we do not collect

- Master password
- Recovery key
- Decrypted logins

## Your rights

You may delete your account and vault ciphertext from settings (API `DELETE /v1/account`).
